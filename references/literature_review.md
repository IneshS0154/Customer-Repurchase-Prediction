# Literature Review — Verification Log (Task 2)

The full review (industry practice, approaches, recent findings, critical comparison table,
research gaps, decision-by-decision justification, and APA reference list) is in
[docs/task1_task2_industry_and_research.md](../docs/task1_task2_industry_and_research.md), §2.

Every reference was checked against the Crossref / DataCite DOI registry for authors, year,
journal, volume, issue, pages and DOI. Claims attributed to each paper are limited to its
published abstract or well-established content. **Before the viva, the person presenting a
task should read the full text of the papers cited for that task.**
